The corporate motto is IT “Con tanta passione, nulla è impossibile.”,
EN “With great passion, nothing is impossible.”, DE “Mit viel Leidenschaft
ist nichts unmöglich.” and FR “Avec beaucoup de passion, rien n'est impossible.”.
Place it visibly in the homepage opening/presentation and shared footer.

The outdoor kitchen/oven line is “Il Fuoco siciliano”, with IT “Fuoco, pietra
e passione. Dalla Sicilia, ovunque.” and EN “Fire, stone and passion. From
Sicily, anywhere.”. Atelier uses IT “Se lo immagini, noi lo costruiamo.”
and EN “If you can imagine it, we can build it.”.

**Why:** The owner supplied these exact brand expressions and explicitly
requested a small text-only change independent of other phases.

**How to apply:** Preserve supplied wording, adapt naturally into all other
site languages including Arabic when active, and preserve the existing design.
Do not turn mottos into technical guarantees or worldwide ordinary construction.

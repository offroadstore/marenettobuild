---
name: Commercial positioning
description: Owner's standing acquisition strategy and limits on portfolio activation.
---

Position Marenetto for high-value work with foreign buyers and well-resourced
local owners. Acquisition is organic only: the owner has not authorized an
advertising budget. The public home is commercial and photographic, not an
article feed.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved this direction for the four-area
reorganization, not a general-purpose blog or paid acquisition campaign.

**How to apply:** Keep useful historic maintenance, emergency, incentives and
permit content online in secondary discovery paths, not primary navigation or
the commercial home. Do not turn the international furniture offer into an
unconditional worldwide building service.

Projects remains a future public destination. Neither illustrative images nor
their technical presence authorize activating a portfolio.

**Why:** The owner requires authentic approved photographs before displaying
Projects, with no disabled menu item or invented case studies.

**How to apply:** Require real materials and explicit approval before promoting
Projects in navigation, home, sitemap or indexing. Publication itself remains
the owner's action.

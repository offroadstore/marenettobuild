For clients abroad, work is organised by SAL (stati di avanzamento lavori).
The architect prepares the computo metrico and divides it into phases.
Each completed phase is demonstrated with photographic documentation and
the report of the geometra or architect acting as direttore dei lavori,
attesting to completion of that phase. Between SAL, when the technician is
absent, the company sends WhatsApp photos, videos and recordings.
There is one company contact; team assistance languages are Italian,
English, Dutch, French and Spanish. Do not promise fixed update frequencies,
name people or guarantee timing.

**Why:** The owner explicitly confirmed this building-site process, distinct
from the property-management partner and Atelier processes, and instructed
that drafting continue without further confirmations.

**How to apply:** Describe SAL and professional attestation separately from
informal company updates. Translate Italian technical institutions with an
explanation, not an invented foreign equivalence. These are owner declarations,
not proof of an internal professional licence, executed project, contractual
payment schedule, twelve spoken languages, or technical review of the guides.

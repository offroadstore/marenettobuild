name: Cefalù panorama constraints
description: Geographical truth and owner's scope limits for the illustrated homepage panorama.
---

The Duomo belongs to the inner town at the foot of La Rocca, behind multiple
rows of houses, not to the waterfront building row. A reference photograph from
a different viewpoint establishes spatial relationships, not screen coordinates.

For the current hero viewpoint, the owner explicitly requires the cathedral
to appear to the LEFT of La Rocca, not centred in front of it.

**Why:** The owner rejected the centrally positioned cathedral in the revised panorama.

**How to apply:** Keep one cathedral on the left of the rock, inland behind roofs,
while preserving the approved foreground. Do not treat an earlier merged image
as visual approval of its geography.

The owner confirmed the locally retouched cathedral-left image on 2026-11-12.
**Why:** This fixes the intended landmark placement for subsequent revisions.
**How to apply:** Preserve that placement; the confirmation is not permission
to publish or replace the foreground.

**Why:** The owner requested correction of the illustrated monument using the
ita.travel Cefalù photograph as a geographic reference only, explicitly excluding
replacement with that photograph or claims to its reuse rights.

**How to apply:** Preserve the approved terrace, stone house, scaffolding,
vegetation, light and composition when revising the panorama. Keep one cathedral,
clearly separated from the sea by roofs. Preserve originals and the existing AI
disclosure; preview correction is not approval of the revision or public release.

AI edits may reframe a nonsquare source even when instructed not to. Do not accept
an apparently plausible whole-scene result as a local retouch.

**Why:** Repeated landscape/detail edits returned square recompositions rather than
the supplied framing.

**How to apply:** Use a square detail input, retain only the required local patch,
and verify unchanged regions against the original before integrating derivatives.
Keep the requested panorama prominent in that square, rather than surrounding it
with mostly empty sky. Even square sky-heavy inputs can produce a zoomed mountain
and oversized cathedral; coordinate instructions alone did not prevent this.
Check spatial alignment against the source before extracting any patch.

Do not treat a tower-only adjustment as completion of a panorama correction.

**Why:** The owner reported that the earlier result appeared unchanged; the
request concerns the relationships of the rock, dense town and waterfront as
well as the cathedral, not additional resolution or a cache explanation.

**How to apply:** Compare against the immediately preceding served revision at
identical viewports, plus enlarged native-detail crops. Assess the whole skyline
and town, while preserving the original foreground and treating visual approval
as separate from technical integration.

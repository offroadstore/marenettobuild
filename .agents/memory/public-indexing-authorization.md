name: Public indexing authorization
description: Owner authorization for verified public editions and the separate exclusion of empty resources.
---

The owner's final clarification authorizes indexing complete, verified public
pages in the twelve original active languages and the subsequently authorized
Arabic edition without another approval, including
services, articles, guides, areas and legal notices. Incomplete or unverified
pages remain noindex. Private access, login and technical/demo/review routes
remain excluded.

**Why:** On 2026-11-25 the owner explicitly replaced the candidate-only indexing
hold. This authorizes inclusion in the release; it does not assert independent
legal, native-speaker, image-rights or human-review certification.

**How to apply:** Keep preview origins noindex and runtime withdrawals/stale
source safeguards fail-closed. Do not activate tracking approval flags, publish,
send real test mail or use IndexNow based on this indexing permission.

Useful, verified professional pages may be indexable, but their empty download
areas must remain public without login and excluded from indexing.

**Why:** The owner distinguishes substantive service information from an empty
archive; broad language/public-page authorization does not waive that boundary.

**How to apply:** Do not promote “files coming” resources through a general
release rule. Reassess them only once authentic, pertinent files are supplied
and the resulting content is checked; never add placeholders to the sitemap.

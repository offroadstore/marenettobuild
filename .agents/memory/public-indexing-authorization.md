The owner's final clarification authorizes indexing complete, verified public
pages in the twelve active languages without another approval, including
services, articles, guides, areas and legal notices. Incomplete or unverified
pages remain noindex. Private access, login and technical/demo/review routes
remain excluded.

**Why:** On 2026-11-25 the owner explicitly replaced the candidate-only indexing
hold. This authorizes inclusion in the release; it does not assert independent
legal, native-speaker, image-rights or human-review certification.

**How to apply:** Keep preview origins noindex and runtime withdrawals/stale
source safeguards fail-closed. Do not activate tracking approval flags, publish,
send real test mail or use IndexNow based on this indexing permission.
